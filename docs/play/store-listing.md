## English

**Name:** Binelik AI: Vent & Swear (28)

**Short description (77):** Let off steam at an AI character who answers back. Voice or text. 18+

**Full description:**

Had a day? Say it. Swear if you need to. A character you pick answers back in its own way: it can argue with you, agree with you, listen calmly or drive you mad by taking the other side.

**21 characters, and two of your own**
- The brawler, the patient one, the pushover, the logical one, devil's advocate, your best mate.
- Your mum, your wife, your husband, your boss, your mother-in-law, your brother-in-law, grandma.
- The football fan, the neighbour with the drill, the civil servant, the influencer, the accountant, the drill sergeant, the taxi driver.
- Make your own character and share it with a QR code.
- Three intensities for every character: mild, medium, heavy.

**Talk or type**
- Voice conversation that keeps going with the screen off, and on Android Auto.
- Or type, with a share card for the best replies.

**Extras**
- Anger meter, scream meter, plates to smash, a fight to rehearse, the week's mood.
- Greek and English.

**Your own AI key, your phone**
- Add your own free Gemini key (or DeepSeek, Groq, OpenRouter). What you say goes from your phone to the AI you chose, under its terms.
- No account. Conversations stay on your phone and are not included in backups. The key is encrypted on the phone.
- A safety check runs on the phone, and the app pauses when a conversation needs a different kind of help.
- 🚩 Report any AI reply from inside the chat.

18+. Not therapy.
